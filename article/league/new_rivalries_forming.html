New Rivalries Forming as the Season Heats Up

4 days ago · Season 1

Three rounds in, and it's already clear this season isn't short of grudges. Some are old, carried over from a title fight that never really ended. Some are brand new, forged in wheel-to-wheel battles that have looked more personal by the lap. Two in particular are shaping up to define Season 1.

Kill3rKai vs Ashigaru — Unfinished Business

This one didn't start this season. Kill3rKai and Ashigaru were the two names at the front of Season 0's title fight, trading blows for both the drivers' and constructors' championships all the way to the final laps. Neither driver has forgotten it. With both of them climbing back through the order in Season 1 and looking increasingly likely to be fighting at the front again, the rivalry that defined last year is very much back on.

Deshy vs XDLegionair — Ferrari's Nemesis

The newer of the two rivalries has already produced some of the tensest racing of the season. Deshy's battles with XDLegionair's Mercedes have been vicious almost every time they've come together — at points it's looked like they're genuinely trying to take each other out rather than just race for position. When they're not colliding, the results speak for themselves: session after session, the two of them have finished within five tenths of one another.

It isn't a one-on-one fight either. With Deshy and Kill3rKai as Ferrari teammates, every clash with XDLegionair carries the weight of a team rivalry as much as a personal one — Mercedes' lone charger against a Ferrari squad that keeps finding itself in the fight, for better and worse.

The Standings Backdrop

All of this is playing out against a title picture that's already shifting. Ashigaru moved to the top of the drivers' championship after round three in Japan, and Red Bull have held the constructors' lead since the chequered flag fell in China. With rivalries this personal running underneath a standings battle this tight, Miami and beyond look set to bring plenty more of the same.
