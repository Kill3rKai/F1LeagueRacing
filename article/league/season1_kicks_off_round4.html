Season 1 Kicks Off — Round 4 Approaches

1 week ago · Season 1

Three rounds into Season 1 and the grid is still finding its shape — three different winners, two championship contenders swapping blows, and a paddock full of drivers with something to prove. Now the calendar turns to Miami, and history says this is a weekend that rarely goes quietly.

Miami Look Ahead

Miami is a track these drivers either love or hate. Few circuits on the calendar split opinion quite like it, and the league's history here backs that up — it's produced some of the most memorable moments of past seasons, for better and for worse.

Last Season's Echoes

The track has a habit of catching people out. Last season Polaris lost his car through the tight chicane during qualifying, a moment that's stuck with him heading back into this weekend. On the other side of it, Kill3rKai secured the win last year, holding off Ashigaru to take the top step.

“It's not my favorite track, but I have high hopes for this weekend.” — Polaris

A Champion Under Pressure

With Ashigaru leading the championship by just 6 points over Antonelli, it's still anyone's game this early into the season. A strong result in Miami could start to open a real gap — a bad one could hand the initiative straight back.

“Race pace feels good, just don't know about the quali pace — might be in for another comeback drive.” — Ashigaru

A Driver With A Passion For Miami

But it's Kai who has a true love and mastery of this track, a level other drivers can only dream of. The question is whether last year's form carries over — after how unlucky he's been to start this season, it's genuinely up for debate whether he'll be the one taking the chequered flag in Miami.

“I've got 150 EUR on Kalshi saying I win this one. I traumatized last year's champion here last season — Ashigaru.” — Kill3rKai

Two Drivers Hungry For The Win

Deshy has something to prove coming into this race too. A phenomenal win at the start of the season set the bar high, but a run of pitstop blunders since has seen him slide down the standings — Miami is a chance to arrest that slide.

XDLegionair is another to watch, chasing back-to-back wins after his dominant drive in Japan. He isn't exactly hiding his intentions heading into the weekend.

“I'm not backing down. I smell blood, and I'm going for the kill.” — XDLegionair

A Driver With Something To Prove

Polaris is also a driver to watch, on something of a redemption arc since last season. With the weight of last year's chicane moment still on his shoulders, the question is whether he can pull out another masterclass like the recovery drives he's already shown flashes of this season.

Excitement Around The Paddock

With every one of these drivers carrying their own Miami story into the weekend, this round is shaping up to be about more than just points. It's going to come down to who can get to the line first.
