Championship Battle Tightens After Round 3

1 day ago · Season 1

Three rounds down, and the top of the drivers' championship has already changed hands. What started as a one-driver runaway has turned into a genuine fight, with the standings shuffling hard after a chaotic weekend in Japan.

Deshy's Early Command

Deshy's Australia win — helped by the fact that almost every other contender DNF'd around him — put him straight into the championship lead after round one, and he wasn't quick to give it up. Even a DNF in the China Grand Prix wasn't enough to shift him off top spot; a strong sprint result there was enough to see him through round two still holding P1.

Ashigaru's Silent Climb

While Deshy held the spotlight, Ashigaru was quietly putting together a very different story. His season has looked just as rough as Kill3rKai's on paper — a DNF of his own in Australia — but back to back comeback drives in China and Japan did the job. By the time the chequered flag fell in Japan, Ashigaru had climbed into the championship lead himself, all without much fanfare along the way.

XDLegionair Closes The Gap

It hasn't been plain sailing for XDLegionair either. Australia brought first-lap chaos and a fight against dirty driving from the Cadillac cars, while China saw him lose traction, spin, and pit early — though he still recovered enough to bank points. Japan was the turning point: a race win there sent him climbing hard up the order, up to fifth in the championship.

The Rest Of The Order Shuffles

Deshy's grip on the top spot finally slipped after Japan, dropping him down to fourth. His teammate went the other way entirely — a podium finish in Japan pushed Kill3rKai from a lowly 17th all the way up to ninth in the standings, one of the biggest single-round climbs of the season so far. Polaris has been climbing too, now up to seventh after a run of solid recovery drives.

Drivers' Championship After Round 3

  1. P1 — Ashigaru
  2. P4 — Deshy
  3. P5 — XDLegionair
  4. P7 — Polaris
  5. P9 — Kill3rKai

Three drivers have now led the championship at some point this season, and the gaps between the top handful are closer than they've ever been. Miami is shaping up to be the round that decides who's actually built to hold onto the lead once they get there.
