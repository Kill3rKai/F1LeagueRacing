Deshy, Strong Start But Strategic Blunders

5 days ago · Season 1

Deshy came into Season 1 off the back of a strong 2025 — a mountain of race wins that put him firmly in the title picture, even if the crown itself slipped away at the last. Expectations were high heading into round one. For a while, he lived up to every bit of them.

High Hopes

A season built on the back of that 2025 form was always going to draw comparisons, and Deshy carried that pressure into Australia knowing exactly what he was capable of on his day.

Starting Off Strong

He didn't waste the opportunity. While first-lap chaos at Albert Park punctured tyres for his teammate Kill3rKai and Red Bull's Ashigaru, and rear diffuser damage ended Polaris's afternoon early, Deshy stayed clear of the wreckage entirely. With most of his closest rivals already out of the equation, the only real threat left was XDLegionair's Mercedes — and Deshy saw him off comfortably, leading every lap to the flag for a commanding win and an early lead in the championship.

Trouble Brewing

China told a very different story. A lack of traction hampered him all weekend, limiting him to fourth in the sprint. The Grand Prix was worse still — the same traction issues never went away, and his race ended in retirement before he could add anything to his tally.

From Great To Worse

Japan started as well as any weekend could. Deshy led the field after qualifying, locking out pole, only for XDLegionair to get past him early in the race. A red flag reset the field and threw the Ferrari pit wall into confusion — Deshy ended up following Mercedes's bold call to bolt on mediums for the restart, a gamble that had him fighting hard to hold position.

Running in XDLegionair's dirty air for lap after lap wore his tyres down faster than expected, and he was eventually forced to pit late, ending any hope of a podium at Suzuka. He crossed the line in eighth — but the stint wasn't wasted entirely. With Deshy out of the way, his teammate Kill3rKai stayed out on worn tyres of his own and kept the fight with XDLegionair alive to the flag, a battle Deshy's early effort had helped set up. The result left Deshy fourth in the drivers' championship heading into round four.

“He does his best to support the team. He did the most for me in japan, i just blundered the win” — Kill3rKai, on Deshy

One win, one DNF, and a podium lost to strategy in the space of three rounds — Deshy's season so far has been as much about what got away as what he's banked. Miami is next, and after a run of strategic blunders, it's shaping up as his chance to bounce back.
