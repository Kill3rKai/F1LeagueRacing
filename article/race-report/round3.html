Round 3 Recap: XDLegionair Takes a Dominant Win

3 days ago · Japanese Grand Prix

The Japanese Grand Prix delivered one of the most chaotic and hard-fought races of the season so far. What started as a Ferrari front-row lockout ended with a Mercedes driver on the top step of the podium, a Red Bull that climbed from fourth, and a Ferrari that refused to go away.

Front-row Ferraris, early drama

Qualifying had set the stage perfectly. Deshy locked out pole for Ferrari, with teammate Kill3rKai lining up alongside in P2. XDLegionair’s Mercedes sat ready to pounce from P3, while Ashigaru’s Red Bull started fourth and Polaris’ McLaren fifth.

On the opening lap the race ignited. XDLegionair lunged past Kill3rKai and immediately set off after Deshy. The two began trading positions in a duel that had shades of Leclerc vs Verstappen at Bahrain 2021 — wheel-to-wheel, neither willing to give an inch. The rest of the field could only watch as the lead group stretched and contracted with every sector.

Red flag resets the strategy

A red flag brought the first phase of the race to a sudden halt. When the field returned to the pits, almost everyone switched to the hard tyre for the restart. Only two drivers took a different gamble: XDLegionair and Deshy both bolted on mediums, banking on pace over longevity.

For a while the mediums looked like the right call. The Mercedes and the lead Ferrari continued their vicious fight at the front, pulling clear of the pack. But the cost of that aggression soon became clear. Deshy’s tyres began to fall off a cliff. Extreme wear forced an unscheduled stop late in the race, dropping him out of the podium places he had fought so hard to defend.

Kill3rKai and Ashigaru close in

Deshy’s misfortune opened the door for his teammate. Kill3rKai, still on the harder compound, found himself in a proper scrap with the race leader. For several laps the Ferrari sat glued to the back of XDLegionair’s Mercedes, looking for any opportunity to retake the lead.

Then came the decisive moment. Kill3rKai took too much kerb, was shunted off track, and lost the position to Ashigaru. The Red Bull driver — who had started fourth and methodically worked his way forward — seized P2 and never looked back.

Mercedes holds on

From there the top three was set. XDLegionair managed the gap to the flag, Ashigaru brought the Red Bull home in a hard-earned second, and Kill3rKai recovered enough to claim the final podium spot in third. Behind them the rest of the field settled into the order that would define the championship picture heading into Round 4.

Final Classification — Top 3

  1. XDLegionair — Mercedes (started P3)
  2. Ashigaru — Red Bull (started P4)
  3. Kill3rKai — Ferrari (started P2)

Three rounds in, the championship already looks wide open. Mercedes has a race win, Ferrari has the pace, and Red Bull has shown it can climb through the field when it matters. Round 4 can’t come soon enough.
